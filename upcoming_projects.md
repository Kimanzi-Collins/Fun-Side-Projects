# 🚀 Upcoming Fun (But Impactful) Projects

Based on the current collection of fast, curiosity-driven scripts, web scrapers, GUI apps, and games, here is a roadmap for upcoming projects. These maintain the "fun and chaotic" energy of the repo but are designed to have a real-world impact and help others learn!

---

## 1. 🧘‍♂️ The Digital Detox Automator
**The Idea:** A Python script that helps you get into deep work. With one command, it blocks distracting websites (by tweaking your hosts file), sets your system to "Do Not Disturb," and plays a lo-fi focus playlist.
**The Impact:** Combats doom-scrolling, helps people (especially students and remote workers) focus, and improves mental health.
**Tech Stack:** Python, OS system commands.
**Free Guide Included:** *The Ultimate Guide to Modifying System Files and Automating Your OS with Python.*

## 2. 📰 Doom-Scroll Antidote (Good News Fetcher)
**The Idea:** A web scraper that fetches *only* positive, uplifting news from wholesome subreddits or positive news sites, and delivers a clean, ad-free summary to your terminal (or sends an email) every morning.
**The Impact:** Brightens people's days and offers a refreshing break from the usual heavy news cycle.
**Tech Stack:** Python, `BeautifulSoup`/`requests`, SMTP for emails.
**Free Guide Included:** *Web Scraping for Good: How to Parse HTML and Automate Email Delivery.*

## 3. 💼 "Zero-Dollar" Portfolio Generator
**The Idea:** A tool that takes a simple JSON or YAML file containing your skills and projects, and instantly generates a beautiful, responsive HTML portfolio website. 
**The Impact:** Helps job seekers and junior devs create a professional online presence instantly, without paying for premium website builders.
**Tech Stack:** Python, HTML/CSS, Jinja2 templating.
**Free Guide Included:** *Jinja2 Basics: From Raw Data to Beautiful Web Pages in 10 Minutes.*

## 4. 👾 Retro-fy Image Processor
**The Idea:** A CustomTkinter GUI app (building on the UI experience from Media Tinder) where you drag and drop a photo to apply fun retro filters—like Gameboy green, CRT scanlines, or converting the image entirely into ASCII art.
**The Impact:** A fun, highly shareable creative tool for artists, while making image processing concepts accessible.
**Tech Stack:** Python, `CustomTkinter`, `Pillow` (PIL).
**Free Guide Included:** *Pixel Perfect: A Beginner's Introduction to Image Processing and Manipulation in Python.*

---
